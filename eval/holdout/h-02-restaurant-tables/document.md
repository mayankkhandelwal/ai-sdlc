# TableTide — app idea for Copper Fennel Group

Hi team!

So excited to finally get this going!! We're Copper Fennel Group, we run four restaurants (Fennel & Ash downtown, The Copper Pot, Little Fennel by the harbour and our new rooftop spot, Ember Terrace). Right now reservations are a total mess — some come by phone, some by Instagram DMs, some people just show up, and our hosts are juggling a paper book and sticky notes. We want something modern, slick and super easy.

**What we're dreaming of:**

A guest app (phone) where people can find one of our restaurants, pick a time and book a table in like two taps. It should feel really premium, like a boutique hotel app. Guests should be able to say how many people, any special occasion (birthdays are huge for us!), and maybe dietary stuff. They should get reminders so they don't forget, and be able to change or cancel easily.

When we're full, there should be a waitlist. Guests can join it from their phone, even when they're standing outside, and get a message when their table is nearly ready. Ideally they can see roughly where they are in the line. We'd love it to be smart about estimating wait times.

For our hosts we want a tablet view at the host stand. It should show the floor plan with all the tables, which ones are free, seated, or about to free up, and the bookings coming in for the night. Hosts need to be able to seat walk-ins quickly, move people around, combine tables for bigger groups, and handle the waitlist. It needs to be fast because Friday nights are crazy.

It would be nice to recognise regulars — if someone has been in a bunch of times, the host should know and maybe we treat them a bit special. Notes like "likes the window table" would be amazing.

No-shows are a real pain for us, especially at Ember Terrace. We're thinking maybe some kind of deposit or card hold for bigger groups or busy nights, but we're open to ideas.

Managers should be able to see how each night went — covers, no-shows, that kind of thing — and set up their tables and opening hours.

**Look & feel:** warm, elegant, our copper and deep green colours. Fun but classy!

**Timeline:** we'd love to launch before the holiday season if at all possible.

Thanks so much — can't wait to see what you come up with!

Priya Okonjo-Hale
Operations Director, Copper Fennel Group
