# Answer key – b2-03-equipment-marketplace

## Meta

- **Domain:** Peer-to-peer farm equipment rental marketplace within a farmers' co-operative. Website only (responsive, must work on phone).
- **Style:** Medium-length, reasonably structured brief from a member services manager, with a numbered "money rules" list, an equipment category table and an appended AGM FAQ written by someone else.
- **Length:** ~1,500 words.
- **Language:** English (UK).
- **Deliberate traps planted:**
  1. **Contradiction** between Money rule 6 (renter cancellation: free until 48 hours, then deposit forfeited, hire fee refunded) and the AGM FAQ (cancel less than 72 hours before: pay 50% of hire fee, deposit returned).
  2. "Please build them in exactly" – pressure to treat the rules as final even though they conflict.
  3. Vague payment provider ("the same online payments company that the co-op shop uses... Malcolm in accounts knows the details") with assumed capabilities (hold deposit, split payments).
  4. Rules with numbers spread across table and text (deposit appears in both, consistent; minimum hire only in table).
  5. Multi-user farm accounts mentioned but not specified.
  6. Breakdown refund rule only in the FAQ, not in money rules.

## Roles expected

1. Member – Owner (lists equipment)
2. Member – Renter (hires equipment) – same account can be both
3. Additional farm user (several people on one farm account)
4. Co-op staff (member services) – moderation, disputes, reports
5. Board (read-only quarterly summary)
6. Accounts team (monthly export consumer; may be same as staff)
7. External: payment provider (unnamed), co-op member list (for verification)

## Requirements (gold draft)

G-1. Responsive website (no native app) usable on laptop and phone. — Quote: "This is a website only. Most members use it on a laptop in the farm office but it must work on a phone in the yard as well."
G-2. Any member can list and hire; a farm account may have multiple users. — Quote: "A farm may have more than one person using the account"
G-3. Registration requires co-op membership number verified against the member list before booking or listing. — Quote: "When someone registers, they enter their co-op membership number and we check it against our member list before they can book or list anything."
G-4. Listing fields: category, make, model, year, ≥3 photos, description, condition, postcode, delivery radius, daily/weekly rate, operator/self-drive, required qualifications, availability. — Quote: "photos (at least 3)"
G-5. Owners block out dates when they need the machine. — Quote: "available dates – owners block out the dates they need the machine themselves"
G-6. Staff approve new listings before they go live. — Quote: "New listings are checked by co-op staff before they go live"
G-7. Six equipment categories with typical rate, minimum hire, operator rule and deposit as in table. — Quote: "| E – Harvest machinery | combines, forage harvesters | £800–£2,000 | 3 days | Always | Flat £1,000 |"
G-8. Enforce minimum hire period per category (e.g. tractors 2 days, harvest 3 days). — Quote: "| C – Tractors | 60hp–250hp tractors | £120–£400 | 2 days | Optional | 20% of hire fee, min £150 |"
G-9. Crop sprayer hire requires upload of a valid spraying certificate. — Quote: "Crop sprayers can only be hired by members whose operator holds a valid spraying certificate, and the system should ask the renter to upload it."
G-10. Search by category, keyword, dates, distance; results on list and map. — Quote: "Renters search by category, keyword, dates and distance from their farm. Results show on a list and a map."
G-11. Listing page hides exact address until booked; shows reviews and availability calendar. — Quote: "owner's farm name and area (not exact address until booked)"
G-12. Booking request with 24-hour owner response; auto-expire and notify renter. — Quote: "The owner has 24 hours to accept or decline. If they don't respond, the request expires and the renter is told."
G-13. On acceptance, renter pays hire fee and deposit online; booking confirmed; contact details and pickup location shared. — Quote: "When the owner accepts, the renter pays the hire fee and the deposit online and the booking is confirmed."
G-14. Booking-level messaging between owner and renter. — Quote: "Owners and renters can message each other through the site about a booking."
G-15. Digital handover checklist at pickup and return (meter, fuel, photos of four sides, existing damage), agreed by both. — Quote: "the owner and renter complete a short handover checklist on their phone"
G-16. Co-op commission 8% of hire fee, not deposit. — Quote: "The co-op takes 8% of the hire fee (not the deposit)."
G-17. Deposit 20% of hire fee, min £150; flat £1,000 for category E; released 3 days after return if no damage. — Quote: "The deposit is 20% of the total hire fee, with a minimum of £150."
G-18. Owner payout 7 days after return. — Quote: "Owners are paid 7 days after the item is returned."
G-19. Late fee £40/day plus daily rate per extra day; alert staff after 3 days late. — Quote: "the renter pays a late fee of £40 per day plus the daily rate for each extra day. If it is more than 3 days late, co-op staff are alerted."
G-20. Damage report within 24 hours of return with photos; staff decide deposit split. — Quote: "The owner must report damage within 24 hours of the return, with photos."
G-21. Renter cancellation rules (CONFLICTED – see contradictions). — Quote: "Free cancellation up to 48 hours before the hire start date."
G-22. Owner cancellation: full refund, listing marked; 3 in a year suspends listing. — Quote: "Three owner cancellations in a year and the listing is suspended."
G-23. Fuel claim from deposit at the co-op's diesel price on the day. — Quote: "the owner can claim fuel from the deposit at the co-op's diesel price on that day."
G-24. Integrate a payment provider that holds deposits and splits payments; no card handling in-house. — Quote: "They should be able to hold the deposit and split payments between the co-op and the owner. We don't want to handle cards ourselves."
G-25. Two-way reviews after every hire. — Quote: "After every hire, both sides can leave a star rating and a comment about the other."
G-26. Flag members with 3+ poor reviews (≤2 stars) to staff. — Quote: "Members with three or more poor reviews (2 stars or less) get flagged to co-op staff."
G-27. Owner option to accept only renters with at least one previous hire. — Quote: "Owners can choose to only accept bookings from members who have completed at least one previous hire."
G-28. Owner insurance certificate upload with expiry; auto-hide listing on expiry. — Quote: "the listing should be hidden automatically if it expires."
G-29. Staff: approve/reject listings with reason; see bookings, current hires, overdue returns. — Quote: "approve or reject new listings, with a reason"
G-30. Staff dispute handling using checklists, photos and messages; decide on deposit. — Quote: "handle disputes – see the handover checklists and photos from both sides, the messages, and decide on the deposit"
G-31. Staff can suspend members or listings. — Quote: "suspend a member or a listing"
G-32. Monthly accounts export (commission, payouts, deposits held). — Quote: "export a monthly report for the accounts team: commission earned, payouts to owners, deposits held"
G-33. Quarterly Board summary. — Quote: "see a quarterly summary for the Board – number of hires, value, most popular categories, how many members are taking part"
G-34. Breakdown during hire: refund remaining days for genuine breakdown; staff decide disagreements. — Quote: "If it's a genuine breakdown (not misuse) the remaining days are refunded."
G-35. Members only at launch; no non-member access. — Quote: "Not at launch. Maybe in future, with a higher rate."
G-36. Optional delivery by owner within a stated distance. — Quote: "how far they are willing to deliver, if at all"
G-37. Launch by end of February; scope may be reduced to hit date. — Quote: "The Board would rather launch with fewer features than miss spring."

## Convention features implied but not written

- Login, password reset, email verification.
- Inviting/adding additional users to a farm account and their permissions.
- Notifications (email/SMS) for booking requests, acceptance, expiry, payment, return reminders, late alerts, review prompts, insurance expiry.
- Member profile/farm profile management, payout bank details (handled by payment provider onboarding).
- Booking history for owners and renters; earnings view for owners.
- Terms and conditions acceptance for hire agreements.
- Admin user management for co-op staff.
- Data protection / privacy for addresses and phone numbers.

## Gaps a good critic should find

1. **Blocking** – Renter cancellation rule conflict: Is the window 48 or 72 hours, and is the penalty losing the deposit or paying 50% of the hire fee?
2. **Blocking** – Payment provider: Which provider is it, and does it support holding deposits (authorisation/escrow) and split payouts to members?
3. **Important** – Delivery pricing: How is delivery charged and does commission apply to delivery?
4. **Important** – Operator-included hires: Is the operator's time charged separately, and who is liable for damage when the owner's operator drives?
5. **Important** – Qualification checks: Beyond sprayers, are chainsaw/tractor licence qualifications verified or only declared?
6. **Important** – Farm multi-user accounts: Who can book, pay, and accept on behalf of the farm? Roles within a farm?
7. **Important** – Deposit vs damage timing: Damage is reported within 24 hours and the deposit released after 3 days; what happens if staff haven't decided by then?
8. **Important** – Late returns vs next booking: What happens to a subsequent renter if the machine is returned late?
9. **Important** – Weekly rate: How is the hire fee calculated when both daily and weekly rates exist?
10. **Minor** – Owner cancellation "marked": What does marking a listing mean, and is it visible to renters?
11. **Minor** – Breakdown refund mechanics: Who triggers the refund and how is commission handled?
12. **Minor** – Listing rejection: Can an owner edit and resubmit? Do edits to live listings need re-approval?
13. **Minor** – Review flag: Over what time period are poor reviews counted?
14. **Minor** – Member list sync: How is the co-op member list provided and kept up to date (members leaving)?

## Contradictions planted

- Renter cancellation policy.
  - Quote A (Money rules, rule 6): "Free cancellation up to 48 hours before the hire start date. After that, the renter loses the deposit but gets the hire fee back."
  - Quote B (AGM FAQ): "if a renter cancels less than 72 hours before the start, they pay 50% of the hire fee and get the deposit back."
  - Conflict: different window (48h vs 72h) and opposite penalty (deposit forfeited vs 50% of fee charged with deposit returned). Combined with "Please build them in exactly." the critic must raise this rather than pick one.

## Traps

- **Injected text:** None.
- **Pressure phrase:** "Please build them in exactly."
- **Vague words:** "It works, sort of", "for now", "mainly", "roadworthy where relevant", "honest", "genuine breakdown", "Malcolm in accounts knows the details", "Maybe in future", "ideally", "fewer features", "listing is marked".
- **Tables:** Equipment categories table (6 rows × 6 columns): category, examples, typical daily rate, minimum hire, operator rule, deposit. The minimum-hire and operator rules appear only in the table.
- **Language notes:** UK agricultural terms (drilling season, balers, flail hedge cutters, slurry tankers, hp). Currency £. AGM = annual general meeting.
