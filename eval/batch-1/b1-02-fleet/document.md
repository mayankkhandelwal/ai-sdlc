Subject: Workshop tracker thing - what we need

Hi all,

Following on from the call last week, here's a rough write-up of what we're after. Sorry it's a bit all over the place, I've done it between other jobs.

So we're Corrandale Haulage, we run about 140 vehicles out of two depots (Mossbridge and Kelling Road). Mix of artics, rigids and a load of vans. Right now the workshop runs everything off a whiteboard, a shared Excel called "FLEET MASTER v7" and a lot of shouting. Drivers report defects on paper walkaround sheets which half the time end up in the cab footwell. We've had two near misses on MOTs this year which frankly can't happen again.

What we want is a simple web system, we've been calling it Spanner Board internally, that the workshop lads and the drivers can both use. Needs to be fast and easy, the drivers aren't going to faff about with anything complicated.

Drivers:
- do their daily walkaround check on it (tyres, lights, mirrors, fluids, etc.) instead of the paper sheet
- report a defect with a photo
- see if their vehicle is OK to go out or not

Workshop:
- see all the defects coming in, sort them out, mark them fixed
- book vehicles in for services, MOTs, tacho calibration, LOLER on the tail lifts etc.
- keep a history for each vehicle - what was done, parts, who did it
- get warned before stuff is due. MOT especially. Ideally a few weeks before but whatever makes sense
- track parts used, maybe stock levels if it's not too much hassle

Some defects mean the vehicle has to come off the road straight away (brakes, steering, that kind of thing) and some can wait till the next service. Workshop needs to be able to say which is which and the vehicle should show as off road until it's signed off again. Not sure who should be allowed to sign it back on - probably the workshop supervisor, or Dave, or whoever's on. We can sort that later.

Transport office (that's Priya's team) would want to see which vehicles are available each morning so they can plan routes. They don't need to edit anything really, just look. Maybe some reports for the monthly meeting, costs per vehicle, number of defects, that sort of thing.

We'd like it to work on the drivers' phones through the browser, no app store stuff. Workshop will mostly use the PCs in the office and a tablet on the shop floor. Signal in the Kelling Road yard is rubbish so bear that in mind.

Also it'd be good if it could pull in mileage somehow. We've got trackers on most of the trucks from a telematics company but I don't know if they have an API, I'll have to ask.

Existing data is all in the Excel so we'd need that loading in at the start.

Budget's not huge so keep it simple. We'd like something in place before the DVSA visit in the new year if possible.

Any questions shout.

Cheers,
Gareth Pryce
Fleet & Workshop Manager, Corrandale Haulage
