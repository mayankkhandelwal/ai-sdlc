# Hollin Vale Academy – Canteen Pre-Ordering ("Trayline")

Requirements outline from the School Business Office
Draft 3 – please treat as a working document

## Background

Hollin Vale Academy is an 11–18 secondary school with around 1,150 students in Years 7 to 13 and about 140 staff. Our canteen is run in-house by our catering team (we call it the Hollin Kitchen) and serves breakfast from 8:00 and lunch in two sittings, 12:20 and 13:00.

At the moment lunch pre-ordering works on paper. On Monday morning each tutor group hands in a brown envelope with a pre-order slip for each student who wants a hot meal that week, ticking the days and the meal option, plus cash or a cheque. The tutor counts the money and the envelopes go to the Business Office, where Mrs Fenwick types the totals into a spreadsheet and prints a daily tally sheet for the kitchen. Students who haven't pre-ordered can still queue for the "grab and go" counter, but the hot meal often runs out by the second sitting. We lose envelopes, cash goes missing, and the kitchen throws away a lot of food because the numbers are wrong once students change their minds.

We want to replace the envelopes with an app called Trayline that parents and students can use on their phones, plus a web dashboard for the kitchen and the Business Office.

## Who it's for

- **Parents and carers** – top up money, order meals, see what their child has eaten, manage allergies.
- **Students** – see the menu, place orders, show a code at the till.
- **Kitchen staff** – see daily numbers, print or view the lists for each sitting, mark meals as collected.
- **Business Office** – manage money, refunds, free school meals, reports.
- **Head of Catering** (Mr Adeyemi) – manage menus and prices.

## Ordering

Parents and students should be able to see the menu for the next two weeks. Each day there are three hot options, a jacket potato bar and a "grab and go" bag. Orders can be placed for any day in the two-week window.

Orders must be placed by 6:00pm the evening before. After that, the order for that day is locked and the kitchen uses the numbers to prepare. This is the most important rule for us because it's what stops the waste.

Students in Years 7 to 13 can log in with their school account and order their own lunch, using the balance their parent has topped up. Parents can see everything their child orders and can block certain items (e.g. no fizzy drinks) or set a daily spending limit.

Each order is for one sitting. Students choose 12:20 or 13:00 when they order, but Year 7 and Year 8 always eat at 12:20, so they shouldn't be offered the later sitting.

If a student is absent, the parent should be able to cancel the day's order and get the money back onto the balance. If they forget, the meal is charged as normal.

Free school meal (FSM) students get a daily allowance of £2.85. If they choose something cheaper the difference does not carry over, and if they add extras (e.g. a juice) the extra is taken from their normal balance.

## Allergies and dietary needs

Each student's allergy information comes from our MIS (the school's student information system – we use a product called ScholarDesk). Allergens must be shown on every menu item using the 14 standard allergens. If a student has a recorded allergy, the app should warn them and block any item containing that allergen. Parents shouldn't be able to override the block themselves – they need to contact the school nurse.

Dietary preferences (vegetarian, halal, no pork) are set by the parent and are just used to filter the menu, not to block.

## Money

Parents top up through the app by card. Minimum top-up £10. Balances belong to the student and carry over between terms. If a student leaves the school, any remaining balance is refunded to the parent on request by the Business Office.

Families with more than one child at the school should be able to top up once and split it between children, or top up each child separately.

We still have some parents who will want to pay cash. The Business Office will need a way to add cash top-ups manually and print a receipt.

## Kitchen dashboard

The kitchen needs a screen (we'll put it on a big monitor in the kitchen) showing, for today and tomorrow:

- How many of each item have been ordered, split by sitting.
- Allergy and special diet orders highlighted, with the student's name and photo.
- A live count of how many have been collected.

Parents can still order up to 9:00am on the day itself; the kitchen just needs final numbers by 9:15 so they can start cooking. The dashboard should update automatically when late orders come in.

At the till, the student shows a QR code in the app (or says their name if they've forgotten their phone, and the till staff look them up). The till staff tap "collected". If a student didn't order, they can still buy from the grab and go counter if there's anything left, paying from their balance.

## Ordering for students (who can order)

To keep things simple and to stop students spending their parents' money on snacks, only parents and carers will be able to place orders. Students will be able to log in to view their orders for the week and show their QR code at the till, but they can't place or change orders themselves.

## Reports

The Business Office needs:

- Daily and weekly takings, split by card top-ups and cash.
- FSM usage per student (we have to report this to the local authority each term).
- Waste report: items prepared vs items collected per day (kitchen will enter the number prepared).
- A list of students with negative or low balances (under £5) so we can send letters home.

## Other bits

- Notifications to parents when the balance is low and when the new menu is out.
- Should work on iPhone and Android. The kitchen and office will use the web version on school PCs.
- Must work for parents with more than one child, and for separated parents who both want access to the same child.
- We'd like to go live at the start of the spring term. We'd run the paper envelopes alongside for the first half-term.
