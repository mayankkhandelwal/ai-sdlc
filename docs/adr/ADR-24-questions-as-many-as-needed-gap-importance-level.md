# ADR-24 · Questions: as many as needed, gap importance levels, check-in every 4 batches, AI answer map confirmed by the user

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Source:** `docs/architecture/06-decisions-and-risks.md` (6.1); audits in `docs/audits/`

## Decision

Questions: as many as needed, gap importance levels, check-in every 4 batches, AI answer map confirmed by the user

## Rejected options

Hard caps; unlimited with no check-in; script-only answer parsing

## Consequences

No endless loops; messy answers handled
